MeerTime Data

MeerTime data will be released according to its data release policies:
  • Data from the Thousand Pulsar Array project will be released as soon as it is validated.
  • All publications will release the arrival times and profiles used to derive the science.
  • All data will be made available in annual data releases within 18 months of the end of the semester.
  • 100% of the data will be stored in the SARAO archives and ultimately released to the community.
 MeerTime data is stored both in the official MeerKAT archive and from the OzSTAR supercomputer at Swinburne University of Technology.

The Thousand Pulsar Array program on MeerKAT – IX. The time-averaged properties of the observed pulsar population
B. Posselt et al. MNRAS 520, 4582–4600 (2023)

The online Tables 1, 2, 5, and 6 are available as supplementary material in csv format with names Census Table1.csv, Census Table2.csv, Census Table5.csv, and Census Table6.csv, respectively. We  provide the folded pulse profiles of the 1271 pulsars with a peak S/N in the total power >3 in the frequency-averaged data. The files are in PSRFITS format and contain full polarization, eight frequency channels, and 1024 bins, together with the ephemerides we used here. The complete data set is available under the DOI doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7272361. The same data set also includes the observational properties, as well as the measurement results of this paper, namely Tables 1, 5, and 6 in csv format.

The Thousand-Pulsar-Array programme on MeerKAT – XIII. Timing, flux density, rotation measure, and dispersion measure time series of 597 pulsars
M. J. Keith et al. MNRAS 530, 1581–1591 (2024)

The data tables and figures in the TPA data release are available at doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8430591. Profile data, ToAs, and other automatically produced data products can be obtained from the MeerTime Portal (https://pulsars.org.au). We request that authors cite this paper and include the zenodo DOI when making use of the TPA data release in future publications.

The first single pulse paper, Paper 8, has an extreme volume of data:

The Thousand-Pulsar-Array programme on MeerKAT – VIII. The subpulse modulation of 1198 pulsars
X. Song et al. MNRAS 520, 4562–4581 (2023)

The data underlying this article will be shared on reasonable request to the corresponding author. 

MeerTime members can access the database from here with appropriate credentials: pulsars.org.au

Public access is being organised for data > 18 months old by removing the need for usernames and passwords. Target date for this is Nov 29 2024.
